Sound-clue families (形声字)

Each group shares one component that gives the SOUND. A different piece gives the MEANING. Learn the group once and you get several characters at a time.

Meaning-clue families (radicals)

These do not share a sound. They share a MEANING radical. Once you know a radical you can guess what a new character is about before you can say it.

Character structures (汉字结构)

Before you learn the parts, learn the shape. Every character is laid out in one of these ways. Red lines are a wrapping part. Green blocks are the pieces inside. Characters shown in red are in this lesson, tap them.
